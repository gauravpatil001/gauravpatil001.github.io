<p class="about-lede">I’m Gaurav Patil, an engineer interested in the systems, decisions, and disciplined practice behind the built environment.</p>

My work sits at the intersection of BIM, multidisciplinary engineering, and construction. I’m drawn to the parts of engineering that reward both technical depth and a clear view of the larger system: how disciplines coordinate, how information moves through a project, and how thoughtful methods turn complex work into repeatable practice.

This site is where I document what I learn. I write practical, experience-based essays about engineering, professional exams, and the habits that make ambitious goals more manageable alongside a full-time career.
